# Bellabeat Smart Device Usage Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes smart-device activity data to understand users' daily activity patterns and identify opportunities for Bellabeat, a wellness company, to encourage a more active lifestyle.

The analysis focuses on daily steps, hourly activity, weekday patterns, and differences in activity levels among users.

## Business Objective

To understand how people use smart devices to track their activity and recommend ways Bellabeat could improve user engagement through personalized wellness features.

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQL (Google BigQuery):** Data exploration, aggregation, and activity pattern analysis
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data validation, PivotTables, calculations, and visualizations
- **PowerPoint:** Presentation of findings and business recommendations

## Dataset Overview

- **Records analyzed:** 457 daily activity records
- **Unique users:** 35
- **Date range:** March 12 – April 12, 2016
- **Key metrics:** Steps, distance, calories burned, activity minutes, and sedentary time

The analysis also used hourly step data to explore how activity varied throughout the day.

## Analysis Process

1. **Data Exploration:** Reviewed the dataset structure, columns, record counts, and date range.
2. **Data Validation:** Checked missing values, duplicate user-date records, zero-step records, and distance inconsistencies.
3. **SQL Analysis:** Calculated activity statistics, classified activity levels, compared weekdays, and analyzed user groups.
4. **Excel Analysis:** Used PivotTables and calculations to summarize activity patterns.
5. **Visualization:** Created charts to communicate the key findings.
6. **Recommendations:** Identified potential opportunities to encourage more consistent physical activity.

## Key Findings

- **Average daily steps:** Approximately 6,547 steps.
- **Low-activity records:** 43.1% of recorded days had fewer than 5,000 steps.
- **Moderate-activity records:** 29.1% had between 5,000 and 9,999 steps.
- **High-activity records:** 27.8% had 10,000 steps or more.
- **Hourly activity:** Average step counts peaked around noon and 7 PM.
- **Weekday patterns:** Tuesday had the highest share of low-activity records at 52.1%, while Wednesday had the lowest at 31.3%.

## Visualizations

The project includes four visualizations:

1. Distribution of Recorded Activity Days
2. Average Steps by Hour of the Day
3. Daily Average Steps Over Time
4. Low-Activity Records by Weekday

These charts help explain activity levels and how recorded movement changes across different times and days.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, Bellabeat could consider testing the following ideas:

- **Personalized step goals:** Encourage users to gradually increase their daily activity.
- **Movement reminders:** Test reminders during less active periods of the day.
- **Weekly progress updates:** Help users review their activity and stay motivated.
- **Personalized wellness suggestions:** Use activity patterns to provide relevant tips and goals.

These recommendations are opportunities to test, not proven outcomes. Further analysis would be needed to measure their impact on user engagement.

## Limitations

- The analysis covers 35 unique users over approximately one month.
- The dataset was collected in 2016 and may not reflect current activity patterns.
- The original case study describes 30 participants, while the analyzed daily activity file contains 35 unique users.
- Zero-step records were retained because their causes could not be confirmed.
- The findings describe the available sample and may not represent all Bellabeat customers.
- The analysis identifies patterns but cannot establish the reasons behind user behavior.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL and Excel can be used to explore activity data, identify patterns, and develop data-driven business recommendations.

The findings suggest opportunities for Bellabeat to test personalized activity goals, movement reminders, and progress updates to encourage a more active lifestyle.
